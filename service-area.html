EXPERIENCE & SERVICE AREA

Experience across Michigan.
Training where the work happens.

Based in Southeast Michigan, B-Ready provides on-site mobile-equipment safety training throughout Michigan. Training is conducted at your facility, using your equipment whenever practical, so operators and trainers learn within the environment where they actually work.

Wayne Michael Bell: an engineer before—and behind—the trainer

Wayne earned a B.S. in Materials Science & Engineering with a Minor in Enterprise from Michigan Technological University and completed advanced graduate materials coursework. His 10+ years in engineering and manufacturing include materials design and characterization, automotive laboratories, metallurgical operations, production problem-solving, electrification manufacturing and engineering project management.

His technical foundation includes finite-element modeling, microscopy, mechanical properties, 8D problem-solving, quality systems and Six Sigma Green Belt certification. Research and invention projects have ranged from an enhanced biomorphic football helmet and pandemic ventilator development to pilot-plant testing for a U.S. Department of Energy-supported microwave steelmaking program.

Experience that scales

Wayne's experience ranges from individual operator evaluations to Train-the-Trainer programs involving as many as 20 trainers in a single engagement. He understands that every facility, workforce and piece of equipment presents different challenges.

Equipment experience

Training experience includes forklifts and powered industrial trucks, narrow-aisle and high-lift equipment, aerial work platforms, overhead cranes, newer electric fleets, laser-guided vehicles and equipment-monitoring systems.

Training brought to your site

On-site delivery connects training to the equipment, traffic patterns, surfaces, work areas, procedures and operating hazards your employees actually encounter.

Programs can include classroom instruction, written testing, practical evaluation, documentation and credentials.

WAYNE MICHAEL BELL • FOUNDER & LEAD TRAINER

Practical instruction backed by engineering and workplace experience.

B-Ready Training was founded by Wayne Michael Bell with a simple goal: provide practical, professional equipment training that helps companies develop safer, more confident operators.

Engineering education and manufacturing career

Wayne earned his Bachelor of Science in Materials Science & Engineering, with a Minor in Enterprise, from Michigan Technological University. He also completed advanced graduate coursework in materials science and engineering, including finite-element methods, thermodynamics and kinetics, crystallography and diffraction, electron microscopy, polymer science, energy materials and mechanical properties.

His engineering and manufacturing career spans more than a decade and includes materials design, automotive central-laboratory work, metallurgical laboratory operations, production support, commercial-vehicle technical service, electrification manufacturing and engineering project management. Along the way, Wayne led 8D investigations, characterized hybrid-motor materials, designed a production metallurgical-laboratory workflow and earned Six Sigma Green Belt certification.

Research, invention and hands-on problem solving

Wayne's research and invention history includes serving as co-principal investigator for an Enhanced BioMorphic Football Helmet project using finite-element modeling and a five-layer impact-energy absorption system. He also led a pandemic ventilator team and helped fabricate and test pilot-plant equipment for a U.S. Department of Energy-supported microwave steelmaking research program.

Wayne Michael Bell has personally delivered operator and Train-the-Trainer programs throughout Michigan in every industry category described on this website.

Training across Michigan workplaces

His experience includes universities, public schools, automotive and engineering facilities, industrial plants, government and correctional operations, sports and entertainment venues, pharmaceutical and chemical manufacturing, environmental testing, construction-material operations and specialized manufacturing environments.

Wayne has worked around traditional forklifts, specialized lift equipment, high-lift turret trucks, aerial work platforms, overhead cranes, newer electric equipment, laser-guided vehicles and equipment-monitoring systems. That background allows him to connect safety requirements with the equipment, conditions and decisions employees face every day.

The B-Ready approach

The goal isn't simply to work through a presentation and hand out a card. Wayne believes operators learn best when they understand why a safety requirement exists, how their equipment actually works and how those principles apply to the conditions they encounter every day.

B-Ready comes directly to the customer's facility, allowing instruction and practical evaluations to take place around the equipment and workplace employees actually use. Operator and Train-the-Trainer programs include classroom instruction, written testing, practical evaluation, training documentation and credentials.
